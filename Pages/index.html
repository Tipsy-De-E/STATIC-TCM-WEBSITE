01 / About TCM

Communication Meets Possibility.

Technology and Communication Management (TCM) is the focus of this promotional campaign. This portfolio aims to help prospective students explore the connection between communication, technology, creativity, and project work.

01

Our Mission

To promote awareness of TCM through creative communication strategies and digital media that help students discover and explore the course.

02

Our Vision

To create an engaging online platform where students can learn about TCM, appreciate the importance of communication and technology, and find reliable information about studying at USTP.

09 / Website Folder Guide

Where Everything Lives.

The active TCM portfolio lives in one HTML file, with its styles and images kept in separate folders. Older campaign files are archived for review.

Activity-Campaign/
├── Assets/
│   ├── tcm-campus-background.png
│   ├── tcm-favicon.png           circular browser-tab logo
│   ├── tcm-logo.png              VOICES campaign logo
│   └── ustp-logo.png             transparent USTP logo
├── Pages/
│   └── Index.html                 all sections and navigation in one file
├── Styles/
│   └── tcm-home.css              violet and USTP themed page styles
└── Legacy-Archive/               previous campaign files to review or delete

Pages/Index.html

Contains the header, navigation, every campaign section, and footer together in one file. Each navigation link scrolls to its matching section.

Styles/ and Assets/

Keep the shared page design, campaign logo, USTP logo, favicon, and campus background separate from the HTML.

Run the site

Open Pages/Index.html directly in your browser, or use VS Code Live Server. The section content no longer requires a local server.

Legacy-Archive/

Preserves the older campaign pages and styles separately so you can review or delete them later.
